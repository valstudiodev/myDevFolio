More About Experience

Hi, I’m Valentyn Tkachenko , a passionate Front-End Developer focused on creating clean, responsive, and engaging web experiences. I love turning creative ideas into functional, user-friendly interfaces using HTML , CSS, and JavaScript. Right now, I’m sharpening my JavaScript skills and exploring modern tools to bring even more interactivity to my projects.

I believe that good design is more than just visuals — it’s about usability, logic, and flow. My goal is to craft websites that not only look great but also perform smoothly across all devices.

When I’m not coding, you’ll probably find me playing basketball, enjoying nature, fishing, or diving into video games. I’m also an animal lover — I share my space with a very curious cat who often supervises my coding sessions.
